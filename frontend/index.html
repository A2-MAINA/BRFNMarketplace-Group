Customer accounts are for people placing orders. You enter delivery details and accept terms and conditions.

Producer accounts are for organisations supplying food. You provide business details that link to product listings.

In this prototype, successful sign in or registration shows a confirmation message below the button and stores your profile in this browser only. Use the marketplace tab to build your dispatch list while signed in.

If you see an error such as a 403 or "invalid credentials", check that your account exists in the backend or contact the BRFN admin user for this environment.
